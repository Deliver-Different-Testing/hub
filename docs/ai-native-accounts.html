AI-native design

How AI changes Accounts

AI as billing checker

Find defects before release

  • Missing schedule or billing rules
  • Inconsistent charge structures
  • Likely duplicate or missing charges
  • Material changes from prior cycles
AI as collections copilot

Prioritise the right debt

  • Rank overdue debt by risk and value
  • Draft reminder sequences
  • Separate disputes from ordinary lateness
  • Suggest escalation paths
AI as margin analyst

Explain where value leaks

  • Quoted model vs invoiced revenue
  • Revenue vs delivery cost
  • Customer profitability by lane and service
  • Courier settlement trend vs billed yield

If Accounts is AI native, the business gets faster cash, fewer invoice defects, cleaner settlements, and a far sharper read on true profitability.
