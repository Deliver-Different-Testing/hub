AI-native design

How AI changes Delivery

AI as field assistant

Help the courier in the moment

  • Next-best action guidance
  • Site-specific instructions
  • Recipient/contact prompts
  • Risk alerts before arrival
AI as proof validator

Check completion quality

  • Photo quality
  • Signature presence or absence
  • Time/location mismatch
  • Suspicious scan sequences
AI as recovery trigger

Act before service failure hardens

  • Predict likely failure early
  • Prompt dispatcher or courier
  • Draft outbound customer update
  • Suggest reattempt / redirect / hold actions

If Delivery is AI native, the field operation becomes more consistent, more auditable, and far easier to scale.
