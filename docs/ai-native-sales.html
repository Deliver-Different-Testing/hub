Sales

Sales should not just win freight. It should create the right customers, with the right commercial model, configured cleanly enough that operations and accounts are not fixing preventable mistakes after go-live.

Stage outcome

Commercially correct setup from day one

Wrong rates, wrong service windows, wrong billing rules, and wrong expectations all start here. An AI-native sales layer acts as an analyst, pricing copilot, and onboarding gatekeeper before the deal is marked won.

AI-native design

How AI changes Sales

AI as analyst

Pre-sales research before human review

  • Enrich company profile
  • Classify likely service type
  • Suggest similar existing customers
  • Flag likely operational constraints
AI as pricing copilot

Guide commercial decisions

  • Propose the closest service template
  • Suggest rate-card starting points
  • Warn when quote assumptions clash with capability
  • Show likely margin sensitivity
AI as gatekeeper

Stop incomplete onboarding

  • Validate billing contacts
  • Validate service windows and route dependencies
  • Identify required integrations
  • Force explicit exception rules before go-live

If Sales is AI native, the company wins better-fit freight and removes downstream exceptions before the first job is even booked.
